WHO WE ARE

A Movement Built On Collective Action

Founded on September 23, 2020 by Mr. Govind Shukla, InAmigos Foundation is a Section 8 registered NGO based in Chhattisgarh, committed to creating meaningful and measurable social impact across India.

With certifications including 80G, 12A, CSR-1, NITI Aayog registration, and IAF ISO 9001:2015 recognition, we ensure transparency, accountability, and high-quality standards in every initiative.

Through strong partnerships, dedicated volunteers, and community-driven programs, we continue transforming lives one initiative at a time.

WHAT WE DO

Our Key Initiatives

Project SEVA

Distributed 50,000+ meals and clothing to underprivileged communities, ensuring dignity and support to those in need.

Project BACHPANSHALA

Bridging educational gaps through digital literacy, life skills, and academic support for children.

Project JEEV

Feeding and caring for 50+ stray animals daily through our dedicated volunteer network.

Project UDAAN

Empowering women through self-help groups, skill development, financial independence, and menstrual hygiene awareness.

Project PRAKRITI

Promoting environmental sustainability through plantation drives and eco-friendly initiatives, with 20,000+ saplings planted.

Project VIKAS

Enhancing employability through internships and skill development programs, training 30,000+ interns across multiple domains.
